# Pelagica Ocean Science

Pelagica Ocean Science is a student-led research initiative exploring
marine biology and ocean systems, with a particular focus on translating
biological knowledge into biomimetic design.

## Research

We investigate marine organisms, ecosystems, adaptations and biological
mechanisms to generate and organize scientific knowledge.

## Biomimicry Design

We explore how principles discovered through marine research can inspire
new materials, structures, technologies and sustainable designs.

## Research Database

This repository contains a growing, literature-backed database of marine
organisms, biological mechanisms and biomimicry opportunities.

## Principles

- Evidence-based research
- Reproducibility
- Open science
- Scientific uncertainty
- Responsible biomimicry

## Status

Pelagica is currently an independent student-led research initiative.
